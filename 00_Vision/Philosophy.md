# Philosophy

## First Principle

A scientific theory is not a document. A document is merely one representation of a theory. The theory itself consists of interconnected concepts, claims, evidence, formulas, questions and reasoning.

Theory OS treats these as first-class object.

## Thinking vs Knowing

Theory OS separates two fundamentally different activities.

- Reasoning
- Temporary.
- Exploratory.
- Incomplete.
- Creative.
- Knowledge
- Persistent.
- Structured.
- Linked.
- Reviewed.

Only reviewed knowledge becomes part of the theory.

## Human and AI

Humans and AI have different roles.

### Human
- Navigator (input)

### Reviewer
- Strategist (both human and AI)

### Decision maker
- AI and reasoner (output)
- Reasoning engine
- Pattern finder
- Compressor
- Assistant

The AI proposes. The human accepts.

## Knowledge Evolution

Knowledge evolves through cycles.

Reasoning
↓
Extraction
↓
Review
↓
Acceptance
↓
Storage
↓
Compression
↓
Future reasoning

Every iteration improves the theory.

## Compression

Complexity should never disappear. It should become manageable. Compression is not information loss. Compression is creating higher-order structure while preserving traceability. Every accepted object should be traceable back to its origin.

## Context

Context should never depend on one enormous conversation.

Context should emerge from structured knowledge.

This allows reasoning sessions to remain lightweight while theories continue growing.

## Theory as a Living System

A theory is never finished.

It evolves.

Objects change.

Relationships change.

Evidence changes.

Theory OS should support continuous evolution rather than static publication.

## Design Philosophy

- Calm.
- Minimal.
- High information density.
- Maximum clarity.
- Low cognitive load.
- The interface should disappear behind the thinking process.
