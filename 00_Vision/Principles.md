# Principles

### Principle 1

Reasoning is temporary.

Knowledge is permanent.

### Principle 2

Chats are not memory.

Chats are computation.

The Theory Graph is memory.

### Principle 3

Every important insight should become a structured object.

Examples:

- Claim
- Formula
- Value
- Concept
- Question
- Evidence

# Principle 4

No AI output automatically becomes knowledge.

Every extracted object passes through human review.

# Principle 5

Every object should be linked.

Knowledge gains value through relationships.

# Principle 6

Navigation is more important than search.

Users should understand where they are inside the theory.

# Principle 7

Compression is continuous.

Theories should become easier to understand as they grow.

# Principle 8

Documents are generated.

They are never the primary storage.

# Principle 9

Context is constructed.

It is never equal to chat history.

# Principle 10

Every reasoning session has a beginning and an end.

Reasoning Sessions are small.

The Theory is large.

# Principle 11

The user is always in control.

AI suggests.

Humans decide.

# Principle 12

Everything should be traceable.

Every claim should answer:

Where did this come from?

What supports it?

What depends on it?

# Principle 13

Knowledge should survive technology.

The specification should outlive frameworks, programming languages and AI models.

# Principle 14

The architecture comes before the interface.

A good interface is the natural consequence of a good architecture.

# Principle 15

Theory OS is built around scientific thinking.

Everything else is secondary.
